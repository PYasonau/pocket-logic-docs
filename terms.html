The game

Pocket Logic gives you logic puzzles to solve on your own device. You do not need an account. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that unlock extra puzzles or hints. Version 1.0.0 offers no in-app purchases. How ad data is handled is described in the privacy policy.

The daily puzzle is one puzzle chosen from the existing collection for that date. It is not a promise that a newly written puzzle appears every day. The reminder, if you enable it, only tells you that today’s puzzle is ready.

Your progress

Progress stays on the device. Reset all progress removes marks and completions and keeps your theme, language, and rewarded-unlock list. Clearing the diagnostics log is a separate action in Settings. Deleting the app removes the local data, subject to the operating system’s backups.
