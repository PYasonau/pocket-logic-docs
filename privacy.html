Summary

Pocket Logic is a puzzle game. You do not create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your puzzle progress stays on your device.

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app sends device and usage data to Google. This is described below. Some internal test builds do not include the ad SDK; those builds send no ad data.

Data stored only on your device

  • Puzzle marks, completed puzzles, hint counts, unlocked puzzle IDs, theme, language, reminder preference, and the local dates on which you solved puzzles.
  • A diagnostics log of up to 500 events, such as event name, time, puzzle ID, difficulty, and hint count. It contains no puzzle grid, free text, name, or email. It is not uploaded. You can clear it in Settings → View diagnostics.
  • If you turn on a daily reminder, the operating system stores one local notification. No push token is requested.

We do not receive this data. Settings → Reset all progress deletes marks and completions. Deleting the app deletes all of it, except where your operating system keeps a backup.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Pocket Logic shows full-screen ads between some puzzles and optional rewarded video ads. You can choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock extra puzzles or extra hints. Ads are provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC through AdMob.

We request non-personalized ads only: ads are not chosen based on your past behavior or profile. Even so, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID; on iOS, the identifier for vendors. On iOS we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission and do not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • IP address, from which Google may infer approximate location (country or city).
  • Ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, and tapped, and whether a rewarded ad was completed.
  • Diagnostics and performance data of the app and the ad SDK, such as launch time, hang rate, energy use, device model, OS version, and language.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit how often the same ad appears, measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the service secure. Data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not sell your data and we do not receive the data that the ad SDK sends to Google in a form that identifies you.

Consent and your choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message before ads load. Ads are only requested if consent allows it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iOS you can reset the identifier for vendors by deleting the app.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Data stored on your device: necessary to provide the game you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)). It never leaves your device.
  • Storing and reading identifiers on your device and ad data sent to Google in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Fraud prevention and security of ad delivery: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Google may transfer data outside the EEA under the safeguards described in its policies.

Retention

On-device data stays until you reset progress or delete the app. Ad data held by Google is kept for the periods described in Google's policies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and object to processing, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent. Because we hold no account or server data about you, most requests concern Google: you can use Google's tools linked above, or write to us and we will help. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl).

Children

Pocket Logic is not directed to children under 13, and the store target audience is 13 and older. We ask Google to show only ads rated G (suitable for general audiences). The puzzles have no violence, sexual content, gambling, chat, or user posts.

Purchases

Version 1.0.0 offers no in-app purchases. If purchases are added, this policy will be updated before they become available.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date before any change in how data is processed.
