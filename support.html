Support

Pocket Logic is a single-player logic-puzzle game. Version 1.0.0 does not use an account, so there is no password to reset and no cloud save to restore.

On this device

  • Progress is saved automatically on the phone or tablet.
  • Settings → Reset all progress clears marks and completions and cancels a scheduled reminder. Theme, language, and rewarded unlocks stay.
  • Settings → View diagnostics shows a local event log. Clear events deletes that log. It is not uploaded.
  • A daily reminder appears only after you choose a time, and only after two completed puzzles. Turn it off in Settings or in the system notification settings.
  • The free version shows Google ads. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland you can change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Version 1.0.0 has no purchases, so there is nothing to restore from the store.
